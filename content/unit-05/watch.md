# Watch out

Notes from Phineas's kit on things that are easy to mix up or where the slides need care. Each starts with the deck it belongs to.

- (deck 1) The stress slide says "flight/flight/freeze." It means fight/flight/freeze.
- (deck 1) Coping: problem-focused = change the stressor itself. Emotion-focused = change how you feel about it.
- (deck 1) GAS order: Alarm → Resistance → Exhaustion.
- (deck 2) The evolutionary box says "reduce our change of survival." It means chance.
- (deck 3) Agoraphobia is not only fear of open spaces: it also includes crowds, enclosed places, public transportation, and being outside the home alone.
- (deck 3) GAD = worry about everything for 6+ months. Panic disorder = sudden panic attacks and fear of having more.
- (deck 4) Time rules: major depressive disorder = 2 weeks or more. Persistent depressive disorder = 2 years or more.
- (deck 4) Bipolar I = full mania, can include psychosis, more severe. Bipolar II = hypomania, no psychosis, more common.
- (deck 4) If someone has depression plus a manic episode, it's bipolar disorder, not persistent depressive disorder.
- (deck 5) ADHD numbers: 6+ symptoms up to age 16, 5+ for 17 and older; symptoms before age 12; in 2+ settings; 6+ months. Average onset is around age 8.
- (deck 6) Dissociative identity disorder (multiple "alters") is not schizophrenia. Schizophrenia is psychosis: delusions and hallucinations.
- (deck 7) Positive symptoms are things added (delusions, hallucinations), not good things. Negative symptoms are things missing (flat affect, no movement, no motivation).
- (deck 7) The negative symptoms box on your slide also lists "disorganized movement" next to catatonic stupor.
- (deck 8) In the DSM-5, OCD and hoarding are no longer anxiety disorders; they have their own category.
- (deck 8) Obsessions = thoughts. Compulsions = actions (or mental acts). The compulsion's relief is what reinforces it.
- (deck 9) Anorexia = restricting to a significantly low weight. Bulimia = binge eating plus trying to prevent weight gain (vomiting, laxatives, fasting, exercise).
- (deck 10) Don't mix up OCD (obsessions + compulsions) with obsessive-compulsive personality disorder (Cluster C: perfectionism, rigid, needs control).
- (deck 10) Paranoid personality disorder has no psychosis. Paranoid delusions of persecution are a schizophrenia symptom.
- (deck 11) Match the meds: antidepressants = SSRIs (serotonin); anti-anxiety = benzodiazepines (GABA); antipsychotics = block dopamine; mood stabilizer = lithium (bipolar).
- (deck 11) Aversion therapy is the opposite of systematic desensitization.
- (deck 11) Only doctors and psychiatrists can prescribe medication.
